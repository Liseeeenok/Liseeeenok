Document signing with a digital signature

Fullstack Laravel Livewire Bitrix24 CryptoPro

A Bitrix24 application for signing documents with a qualified e-signature through CryptoPro — inside the portal where the documents already live.

The task

Documents were signed outside the portal: download, open a separate program, sign, upload back. Every signature was manual work and one more chance to make a mistake.

My role

Fullstack developer: built the application end to end.

What I built
  • Choosing a digital signature certificate.
  • Signing documents through the CryptoPro plugin.
  • Generating an archive of signed documents.
  • Embedding signing into the portal's business processes.
How it works

A Bitrix24 application on Laravel and Livewire; the signature itself is applied through the CryptoPro plugin on the client side.

Outcome

Signing happens inside the portal: the certificate is chosen where the document is, and signed files end up in an archive.
