Companies, contacts and leads on a map

Fullstack Bitrix24 maps

A Bitrix24 module that shows companies, contacts and leads on a map with clustering and custom icons.

The task

CRM objects have addresses, but there was no overall picture: where clients cluster, where districts are empty, where a new site opened. A list of addresses does not answer that.

My role

Fullstack developer: address geocoding, the map, clustering, filters.

What I built
  • Geocoding CRM entity addresses.
  • Rendering objects on the map with clustering of nearby points.
  • Custom icons per entity type: company, contact, lead, site.
  • Filters and an object card right on the map.
Outcome

A manager sees the geography of clients and the load by district instead of a list of addresses.
