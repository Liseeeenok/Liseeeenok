Companies, contacts and leads on a map

Fullstack Bitrix24 maps

A Bitrix24 module that shows CRM objects on a map: companies, contacts, construction sites and leads.

Problem

There were many objects with addresses, but no way to see them together on a map — and therefore no way to judge a district or its density.

My role

Fullstack developer: address geocoding, the map, clustering and icons.

What I built
  • Geocoding CRM entity addresses.
  • Rendering objects on the map with clustering of nearby points.
  • Custom icons per object type and filters.
Outcome

A finished Bitrix24 module that shows the needed data on a map, groups it and marks it with icons.
