RIS

Frontend October CMS Node.js 4 years

A system for tracking research publications and achievements of IDSTU SB RAS staff: papers, projects, reports and supporting documents.

Problem

Research output was tracked by hand and preparing supporting documents and the annual report took weeks.

My role

Frontend developer: interfaces, new sections and the staff profile. I also collected and coordinated new requirements with the institute's staff.

What I built
  • Reworked the interfaces and added new tabs to the existing system.
  • Built a personal page for each researcher with papers, projects and achievements.
  • Added publication analytics and search filters.
  • Connected automatic generation of author certificates and other documents.
How it works

The core is October CMS on top of Laravel with features shipped as plugins. Documents are generated by a separate Node.js service that pulls data from the system and returns ready files.

Outcome

Preparing supporting documents became about 3 times faster and the annual report about 5 times faster. Verified by the institute's staff using it daily.
