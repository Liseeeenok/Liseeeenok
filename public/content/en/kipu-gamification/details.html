KIPU — gamification

Fullstack Bitrix24

A corporate gamification system: employees earn currency for quests, level up skills and spend what they earn in a store.

Problem

Employee motivation had to be raised: routine tasks and achievements were not tracked or rewarded anywhere.

My role

Fullstack developer: reward logic, personal cabinet, store, metrics.

What I built
  • Custom currencies awarded for completed quests.
  • Employee skills and level progression.
  • Quests with completion conditions.
  • A store where earned currency is exchanged for rewards.
  • Per-employee metrics for managers.
Outcome

A finished corporate gamification system embedded into the Bitrix24 portal.
