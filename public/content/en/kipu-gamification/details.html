KIPU — gamification

Fullstack Bitrix24

A corporate gamification module for the Bitrix24 portal. Inside: currencies, quests, skills, a store and per-employee metrics. I built all of it, from reward rules to the personal cabinet.

The task

Motivation rested on words: routine tasks and achievements were recorded nowhere and bonuses were handed out subjectively. A mechanism was needed where a contribution is visible and the reward for it is transparent.

My role

Fullstack developer: reward logic, personal cabinet, store, metrics for managers.

What I built
  • Custom currencies and rules for awarding them for completed quests.
  • Quests with completion conditions and result checks.
  • Employee skills and level progression.
  • A store where earned currency is exchanged for rewards.
  • Per-employee metrics: who takes part, how they grow, where they stall.
Outcome

A working system inside the portal: an employee sees what earned the currency, a manager sees the overall picture of participation.
