CRM change history

Fullstack Bitrix24

A Bitrix24 module that keeps a change history of the CRM: what changed, when and by whom.

Problem

The CRM was turning into chaos: edits left no trace, and there was no way to tell who changed a deal or a contact and when.

My role

Fullstack developer: change capture, history storage, viewing interface.

What I built
  • Intercepting CRM entity changes and recording history.
  • A viewer that shows who changed what and when.
  • Filters by entity, employee and period.
Outcome

A finished module that makes every CRM change traceable.
