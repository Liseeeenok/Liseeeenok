CRM change history

Fullstack Bitrix24

A Bitrix24 module that keeps the history of CRM changes: who changed what in a record and when.

The task

Edits to the CRM left no trace. When a deal or a contact changed unexpectedly, there was no way to find out who did it and when — the argument was settled from memory and chat logs.

My role

Fullstack developer: change capture, history storage, viewing interface.

What I built
  • Tracking CRM entity changes and recording history with old and new values.
  • A viewer showing who changed what, into what, and when.
  • Filters by entity, employee and period.
  • History stored apart from the working tables so it never slows the CRM down.
Outcome

Any change can be pulled up and shown: disputed edits are no longer settled from memory.
