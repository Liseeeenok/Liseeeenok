Notificat personal cabinet

Fullstack Laravel Livewire

Notificat is a customs broker for electronics. I built the client cabinet end to end, from the data model to the interface.

The task

Clients learned about deal status, documents and invoices by phone and email. Managers forwarded files by hand, payments were reconciled in chat threads, and some documents were assembled per client.

My role

Fullstack developer: the whole cabinet service.

What I built
  • Client deals with goods, statuses and history.
  • Documents per deal: viewing and download.
  • Invoices and payments in one place, no email reconciliation.
  • Document generation from deal data.
  • Sign-in and access separation: every client sees only their own data.
How it works

Laravel with Livewire: server-rendered interface components without a separate SPA; data and files in the database and storage.

Outcome

The cabinet has been in daily use for two years: clients see deals, documents, invoices and payments themselves and generate the documents they need.
