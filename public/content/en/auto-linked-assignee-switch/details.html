Automatic assignee switch in linked entities

Fullstack Bitrix24

A Bitrix24 module that switches the assignee across every linked entity at once.

Problem

When a manager changed, some linked records stayed with the previous employee, and nobody could tell who owns the client now.

My role

Fullstack developer: switch rules, link processing, interface.

What I built
  • Detecting an assignee change on an entity.
  • Automatically switching the assignee in all linked entities.
  • Keeping a change history so the origin of each edit is visible.
Outcome

A finished Bitrix24 module that tracks and switches assignees in linked entities.
