Automatic assignee switch in linked entities

Fullstack Bitrix24

A Bitrix24 module: when the assignee changes, it changes across every linked entity — deal, contact, task and request stay with one employee.

The task

When a manager changed, some linked records stayed with the previous employee. The client ended up orphaned: tasks hung on someone who had left, calls went to the wrong person, and six months later nobody could find where it broke.

My role

Fullstack developer: switch rules, link traversal, change history.

What I built
  • Detecting an assignee change on a CRM entity.
  • Automatically walking linked entities by configured rules.
  • Guarding against repeated processing and cycles in the links.
  • A change history showing where an edit came from and who started it.
Outcome

Relations hold: when a manager changes, the records move together with the client.
