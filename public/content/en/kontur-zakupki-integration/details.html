Kontur.Zakupki integration

Fullstack Laravel Livewire Bitrix24 API

Public procurement integration for Bitrix24: tenders arrive in the portal together with their files, statuses are kept up to date, and the relevant ones are picked by filters and triggers.

The task

Managers searched the website and copied tenders into the portal by hand: files were lost, statuses went stale, and won tenders were found late.

My role

Fullstack developer: the Kontur.Zakupki integration, the Bitrix24 import, filters and triggers.

What I built
  • Import of public procurement from Kontur.Zakupki into Bitrix24 together with all files.
  • Tender status tracking inside the portal.
  • Filters: won tenders only, tenders with results, or by configured conditions.
  • Triggers for specific tenders so an important one is never missed.
How it works

Laravel and Livewire with the Kontur.Zakupki API and the Bitrix24 REST API: data and files are moved into the portal and statuses are synchronised.

Outcome

Tenders, files and statuses live in the portal: a manager sees won tenders without manual exports.
