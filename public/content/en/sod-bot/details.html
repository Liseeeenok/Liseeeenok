SOD bot

Fullstack Python personal project

A resource-farming bot for an online strategy game: it reads the screen and emulates mouse movement to play without a human.

Problem

Farming resources by hand in an online strategy is slow and dull, and plain coordinate macros break on any UI change.

My role

Fullstack developer, personal project.

What I built
  • Recognising in-game elements from the screen.
  • Emulating human mouse movement instead of instant coordinate clicks.
  • Farming resources and levelling skills by a configured scenario.
Outcome

A working bot that reads the screen and farms resources for the player.
