SOD bot

Fullstack Python personal project

A personal project: a resource-farming bot for an online strategy. It reads the screen, understands what is going on and moves the mouse like a human.

The task

Farming resources in a strategy game is hours of identical clicks. Plain coordinate macros break at the first UI change, and nobody plays at night.

My role

Fullstack developer, personal project.

What I built
  • Recognising in-game elements on screen instead of hard-coded coordinates.
  • Smooth mouse movement and pauses — behaviour closer to human.
  • Farming and levelling scenarios that work around obstacles.
  • Error recovery: the bot notices it is stuck and returns to the scenario.
Outcome

The bot runs unattended, farming resources and levelling while its owner is busy with something else.
