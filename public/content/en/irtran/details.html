IRTRAN

Frontend IrGUPS

A simulator for freight logistics paperwork by rail. It reproduces the closed Russian Railways environment so IrGUPS students can practise on a comprehensible copy instead of paper manuals.

The task

The real system runs in a closed environment with no student access, while freight paperwork is a skill learned only by doing. Teachers drew the sequence of steps on the blackboard.

My role

Frontend developer: document screens, reference data, validation.

What I built
  • Step-by-step logistics documents — from request to processing.
  • Reference books for cargo, stations and wagons.
  • Field validation against the paperwork rules: a hint before sending, not an error afterwards.
Outcome

A student walks the whole document flow in the interface, and a teacher sees the result and reviews mistakes.
