IRTRAN

Frontend IrGUPS

A freight logistics system for Russian Railways: a training ground that reproduces the paperwork for delivering goods by rail.

Problem

The real system runs in a closed environment, so young specialists had no place to practise preparing logistics documents.

My role

Frontend developer: document screens and reference data handling.

What I built
  • Screens for creating and processing logistics documents.
  • Reference books for cargo, stations and wagons.
  • Validation of document fields against the paperwork rules.
Outcome

A finished system that models the original workflow of creating and processing freight logistics documents.
