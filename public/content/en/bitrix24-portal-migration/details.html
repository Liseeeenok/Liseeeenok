Bitrix24 portal migration

Fullstack Bitrix24

A module that moves data from one Bitrix24 portal to another while keeping the links between records.

Problem

Moving to a new portal had to keep everything — reference data, deals, tasks, files and relations between them — instead of copying by hand.

My role

Fullstack developer: portal structure analysis, entity and relation migration.

What I built
  • Exporting entities from the source portal and importing them into the target.
  • Preserving links between records and file attachments.
  • Migrating employees and their roles.
Outcome

A finished module that fully transfers a portal's data — records, links, files and employees — to another portal.
