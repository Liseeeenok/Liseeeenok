Bitrix24 portal migration

Fullstack Bitrix24

A module that moves data between Bitrix24 portals: entities, links, files and employees.

The task

Moving to a new portal is usually done by hand: export the reference data, recreate deals and tasks, lose relations and attachments. On a large portal that is weeks of work and guaranteed mismatches between the two systems.

My role

Fullstack developer: portal structure analysis, entity and relation migration, repeatable runs.

What I built
  • Reading source entities page by page — no failures on volume.
  • Transferring into the target portal while keeping relations between records.
  • Migrating files and employees together with their roles.
  • Mapping old to new identifiers so a repeated run creates no duplicates.
  • A migration report: what moved, what was skipped and why.
Outcome

Records, relations, files and employees move completely, and re-running the migration is safe.
