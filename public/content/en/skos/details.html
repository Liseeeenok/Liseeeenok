SKOS

Frontend Russian Railways

A training control system for Russian Railways employees: schedule, attendance and result checks.

The task

In a large company training is scattered across departments: plans live in spreadsheets, attendance is marked on paper, results sit in personal files. Building a picture of who learned what and when by hand is impossible — and without it neither overload nor gaps are visible.

My role

Frontend developer: prototype screens for planning and control.

What I built
  • A training schedule by department and period.
  • Screens for marking attendance at classes.
  • Checking training results per employee.
  • A department summary: who completed the plan and who fell out of it.
Outcome

A prototype used to test scenarios with directorate managers: what has to be visible to plan training instead of compiling reports.
